Nothing here waits under a heat lamp. When you order kothu, the roti hits the iron, the blades start ringing, and the whole shop knows. We ask your spice level before the kitchen ever sees your ticket.

    Turmeric-gold rice, browned onions and cashews, cooked slow with a spice-crusted piece and a boiled egg. Jaffna-deep, not mild.
